# GradeNext Smart Lab

**The virtual laboratory wing of the GradeNext platform** — every science and math concept a US student meets from Grade 1 to Grade 12, visualized, manipulable, and experimentable, in the browser.

## The pitch in four lines

1. **Coverage**: ~164 adaptive-depth simulations spanning Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Earth & Space, Math, and Engineering — mapped to NGSS performance expectations and Common Core.
2. **Experimentation, not animation**: every sim has Explore (sandbox), Guided Lab (hypothesis → measure → conclude, feeding a digital Lab Notebook), and Challenge (goal-based play) modes.
3. **A generation ahead on UX**: one modern, touch-first design system ("LabKit") — versus a market of GeoGebra exports and Flash-era GUIs.
4. **AI-native**: a lab assistant that reads the live experiment state and teaches Socratically through it — the clearest white space in the 2026 market.
