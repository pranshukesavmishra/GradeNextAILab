Engine notes found while reading:
- The test VM (`ms/engine.ts`) has `Float64Array` and `Uint8Array` but **no `Float32Array`**, so
  model code must not use it. Only `sims-g*.js` files load there. Models shared between 7F labs go
  on `L.models[...]` of the earlier lab, and the later one reads them from there.
- `mech.js` is the reaction-mechanism player, not a structures engine. The shake-table frame is
  built in `art-g7f.js` on R3/BENCH.
- `TERRAIN.block` is square (one `size`). The tsunami ocean strip needs a rectangular heightfield,
  to be written in `art-g7f.js`.

Plan (verify the models in a scratch runner first):
- **7F-1 Hazards and Their Reach** (`g7f-hazards`, F1). `geologic`: Joyner–Boore 1981 PGA
  (log A = −1.02 + 0.249M − log r − 0.00255r, r = √(d² + 7.3²)), site factor (760/Vs30)^0.36,
  Worden 2012 MMI, energy log E = 1.5M + 4.8; landslide on an infinite slope (FS with pore water
  and a seismic coefficient) and the Jibson 2007 Newmark displacement. `weather`: SCS-CN runoff,
  Kirpich Tc, SCS unit hydrograph qp = 0.208·A·Q/Tp, Manning stage in a compound channel, levee
  overtopping, a river-valley block with a town. `tsunami`: subduction slip from M0 = μLWD
  (Blaser 2010 L and W), 1D linear shallow-water integration across the ocean, c = √(gh)
  (4000 m → 713 km/h; Chile 1960 → Hilo ≈ 15 h; Tōhoku → Crescent City ≈ 10 h), Green's law
  h^−¼ on the shelf, Synolakis 1987 run-up. `california`: a CA relief block (outline polygon,
  faults, volcanoes, cities) with a per-city profile from the same models. `reach`: the area and
  duration of each hazard, taken from the models and plotted log–log.
- **7F-2 Forecasting and Early Warning** (`g7f-forecast`, F2). `history`: a synthetic G-R
  catalogue (b ≈ 1) and a least-squares b-value. `probability`: Poisson 1 − e^(−t/T) against a
  renewal (BPT) model, with many simulated 30-year futures (the "overdue" trap). `maps`: a real
  PSHA over the CA block (fault sources, G-R rates, a GMPE with lognormal σ) at 10 %/50 yr (475 yr)
  and 2 %/50 yr (2475 yr). `warning`: P at 6.5 km/s, S at 3.5 km/s, station detection plus
  processing, the blind zone and the warning time (SASMEX ≈ 60 s for Mexico City). `inform`: an
  annual-peak flood record, a Gumbel fit, the 100-year flood, 1 − 0.99³⁰ = 26 %, and the choice of
  levee height.
- **7F-3 The Shake-Table Engineering Studio** (`g7f-shake-table`, F3–F5). An n-storey shear frame
  (masses, storey springs, Rayleigh damping), an eigen-solve for its modes, and Newmark-β
  integration under synthetic ground motions (a sine sweep, Kanai–Tajimi filtered noise with an
  envelope, a pulse). Variants: bracing, base isolation (T ≈ 2–3 s), a tuned mass damper
  (Den Hartog f = 1/(1+μ), ζ = √(3μ/8(1+μ)³)). Set-ups as BATCH_PLAN lists them: problem,
  tradeoffs, candidates, score, recommend, model, rounds, optimize. Traps: stiffer is always
  safer; taller always sways more.
