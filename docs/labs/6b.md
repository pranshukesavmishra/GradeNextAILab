## 6B-3 Levels of Organization — `g6b-levels` (B3.1–B3.6)

Set-ups: `hierarchy` (B3.1, B3.6) · `special` (B3.2) · `tissues` (B3.3) · `organs` (B3.4) ·
`systems` (B3.5, B3.6). 38 controls, 22 presets, 6 problems, 6 walkthrough steps, 5 quiz questions.

Models and the published numbers they reproduce (tests in `ms/g6b.test.ts`):
- Cell census of a 70 kg adult, Bianconi et al. 2013: 3.72 × 10¹³ (2.63 × 10¹³ red cells, 71 %);
  counts scale with mass because cell size does not (red cells: mouse 6.6, goat 3.6, elephant 9.2 µm).
- Red cell, Evans & Fung 1972 shape (R0 3.91, C0 0.207, C2 2.003, C4 −1.123): D 7.82 µm, V 94.1 fL,
  A 134 µm² (published 7.82, 94, 135). Narrowest tube at fixed A and V (capsule): 3.02 µm, against
  Canham & Burton's ≈ 2.8–3 µm; a sphere of the same volume: 5.64 µm. A spherocyte family keeps V.
- O₂ loading: each column of the cell loads through its faces as a slab (Fourier series), with one
  assumed constant D_eff = 1 × 10⁻¹¹ m²/s (haemoglobin binding slows the front). Disc t95 = 144 ms,
  sphere 593 ms (ratio 4.1, independent of D).
- Neurons: Hursh 6 m/s per µm (myelinated), √d (bare); synapse 0.5 ms. 1 m at 10 µm: 16.7 ms; a
  chain of 100 µm cells 5.02 s.
- Muscle: active state = sum of twitches t/tc·e^(1−t/tc); force = Fmax(1 − e^(−a/3.48)) so a single
  twitch is ¼ of tetanus; recruitment Φ((V − 4)/1.2); 22.5 N/cm² specific tension → biceps 5 cm²: 112 N.
- Skin: TEWL = Δc/(n·r_layer + r_air), r_layer 683 s/m × 16 layers, r_air 780 s/m → 8.0 g/m²/h intact;
  each tape strip removes 6 % of what is left.
- Tendon: toe to 3 % strain, E 1.2 GPa, UTS 100 MPa → Achilles 65 mm² breaks at 6.5 kN (≈ 9.5 × body weight).
- Stomach: mucus–bicarbonate barrier as a reaction front (acid in, bicarbonate out, 20 mM at the lining
  while secretion allows); one calibrated constant D_gel = 2 × 10⁻¹¹ m²/s, giving a neutral lining to lumen
  pH ≈ 1.4 with 150 µm of gel and 3 µmol/cm²/h. Meal of 400 particles ground by 3 antral waves a minute,
  only < 2 mm pass: t½ 62 min for 8 mm bites (clinical solid t½ 60–90 min); weak muscle leaves 55 % at
  4 h (> 10 % at 4 h is the gastroparesis criterion).
- Whole body on a log clock: O₂ store split between lungs (FRC 2.4 L) and blood (1.005 L O₂ capacity,
  Hill n 2.7, P50 26.8) → breath-hold blackout (SpO₂ 50 %) at 2.9 min, 10.2 min after pure O₂; brain
  O₂ 8 mL at 49 mL/min → unconscious in 9.8 s (syncope 8–12 s); liver out → glucose 2.8 mM in ~1 h;
  gut out → 7 L water lost in 2.8 days; kidneys out → urea 40 mM at 5.9 d, K⁺ 7 at 7 d; no sweating
  while running → 40 °C in ~30 min.

Bugs found while building: drawing the zoom levels in unit coordinates made RX drop all shading
(its effects fade below ~10 px of *its own* r) — levels now draw in a 1000 px reference field and are
scaled; a long thin root lit by RX.volume went grey (radial gradient shorter than the shape — lit
across its thickness instead); `G.tag` called without its ctx; muscle force law first gave a twitch of
½ tetanus (K wrong) — fixed to ¼ with a saturating law; ship's bold-number parser read "billion" as
×10⁹ on a value already in billions.

Upgrade pass 1 (after it worked): stage drag handles for the capillary width and the tendon load as
well as the zoom; a gene-expression card per specialised cell (one genome, different genes on); a
pure-O₂ breath-hold, sweat-gland and body-fat controls on the whole-body model; the landscape plot of
time-to-failure for every organ; a header that no longer claims "nothing fails" when something does.
Upgrade pass 2 (after the sheets): the level ladder compacted to two lines so the animals card never
covers it; the red cell drawn face-on by haemoglobin thickness (pale thin centre) instead of as a ball;
a bigger cut-through profile; neuron tracks moved clear of the gene card; the root on wet filter paper

## 6B-4 The Body Systems Bench — `g6b-systems-bench` (B4.1–B4.6)

Set-ups: `digestive` · `excretory` · `circulatory` · `respiratory` · `muscular` · `nervous`, one per
subtopic, each a classic experiment on the same 3D bench with a plate of the system beside it.
36 controls, 20 presets, 6 problems, 7 walkthrough steps, 5 quiz questions. Figure library `art-g6b-3.js`.

Models and numbers reproduced (tests in `ms/g6b.test.ts`):
- Amylase: Michaelis–Menten × Q10 2 × pH bell (opt 6.8) × denaturation (half-life 5 min at 45 °C, halving
  every 4 °C). Q10 halves the rate 10 °C colder; fastest near 40–45 °C over a 10-minute test, never at
  60 °C, in pH 2 acid or boiled; cold is slow, not dead. Spotting-tile colours from starch left.
- Kidney water load: plasma osm = solute/water, ADH 0.4 pg/mL per mOsm above 282 (Robertson),
  Uosm = 50 + 1150·ADH/(ADH + 0.6), 0.6 mOsm/min to excrete: 0.85 mL/min at rest; a litre of water
  peaks at ~12 mL/min near 90 min and 0.73 L is out in 3 h; saline about a quarter of that; no ADH
  ~17 L/day (diabetes insipidus 3–20 L/day). GFR 125 mL/min = 180 L/day. SG ≈ 1 + 0.000026·Uosm.
- Circulation: CO = HR × SV (70 × 70 = 4.9 L/min), MAP 93, pulse pressure SV/1.5 → 116/70; resistance
  falls as output rises (R ∝ CO^−0.85), so exercise at 15 L/min gives MAP ≈ 110, not 285. Poiseuille
  narrowing 1/(1−n)⁴ in series with the bed: 50 % → 97 %, 80 % → 45 %, 90 % → 5 % of flow (Gould's
  critical-stenosis shape); in exercise the bed dilates and a 70 % plaque passes only 57 % (angina).
  Blood viscosity from haematocrit 4.3 mPa·s at 45 %.
- Bell jar: Boyle in the air round the balloons + balloon compliance; a hole stops all filling; the
  real chest (250 cm², compliance 0.2 L/cmH₂O) takes ~370 mL for 1.5 cm of diaphragm.
- Forearm lever: torque balance with the true line of the biceps (shoulder to a 4 cm insertion):
  5 kg at 90° → 489 N (10× the load); larger near full extension where its moment arm shrinks;
  pushing down loads the triceps (2.5 cm behind the elbow) instead.
- Ruler drop: t = √(2d/g); 19.6 cm = 0.200 s, a 30 cm ruler times to 0.247 s; trials seeded per person,
  cue (sight/sound/touch), texting, sleep and practice shift the mean.

Bugs found: a `//` comment on a one-line loop body commented out the `t += dt` — the kidney run never
ended (a model check hung, out of memory); blood pressure in exercise was 318/251 until resistance was
made to fall with output; the elbow angle was measured the wrong way round (an extended arm pointed up);
the bell jar's glass was an opaque cylinder (now MEAS.beaker glass); the ruler box lay along the bench
(axes) — now BENCH.rule standing up; "86 640" in a working read as two numbers; a page error on a
stage that had not yet been laid out (guarded: no plate on a stage under 160 × 200 px).

Upgrade pass 1 (after it worked): exercise vasodilation in the coronary bed, so the same plaque gives
angina only with effort; a pump-vs-saline-vs-nothing drink choice; ADH none/injected; fibrosis and
emphysema-like lung stiffness; a texting/sleep/practice ruler test with dropped catches; drag handles
on the rubber sheet and on the model hand. Upgrade pass 2 (after the sheets): amylase drawn as a folded
protein with a groove (and an unfolded tangle when boiled) instead of a pac-man disc; real glass jar;
ribs cut through at the chest wall instead of bars across the lungs; a heart-shaped heart; a head in
profile with the path of the signal and an arm; labels shortened or moved so none is clipped; phone
plates kept clear of the stage hint.

## 6B-5 The Body During Exercise — `g6b-exercise` (B5.1–B5.6)

Set-ups: `meal` · `oxygen` · `move` · `balance` · `exercise` · `break`. 33 controls, 18 presets,
5 problems, 6 walkthrough steps, 5 quiz questions. Figure library `art-g6b-4.js` (cycle ergometer,
metabolic cart, gas cylinder, glucometer bench, grip dynamometer with EMG; the body at work, blood-flow
bars, motor-neuron pool, glucose route on the body).

One coupled integrator (1 s steps; 10 s for the heat ride): VO₂ need = 3.6 mL/kg/min + 11.8 mL/min per W,
30 s on-kinetics; VO₂max = HRmax (Tanaka 208 − 0.7·age) × SVmax × (CaO₂ − 50 mL/L); cardiac output
from the Fick floor plus skin flow for heat; HR = CO ÷ SV; ventilation solved by bisection so that
VE = 1.3·863·VCO₂/40 + 2 L/min per mmHg of PaCO₂ above 40 + 0.6 L/min per mmHg of PaO₂ below 60;
lactate made past 60 % of VO₂max, cleared in 10 min; heat 3.5 W per pedal watt, sweat 600 W/°C capped by
humidity; water from sweat and breath, plasma volume → stroke volume; urine falling with water lost and
effort; blood shared between brain (fixed 0.75 L/min), heart 4 %, skin, gut, kidneys and muscle.
Reproduced: rest HR 61 (SV 70), 120 W → VO₂ 1.63 L/min, HR 152; trained rest HR 45; 5 % CO₂ → VE ×3.7
(PaCO₂ 46.5); 12 % O₂ → VE only +17 % with SpO₂ 77 % (hypocapnic brake); flow to muscle ×5–10, gut and
kidneys −30–70 % (Åstrand's redistribution); 2 h at 32 °C, 80 W → ~1 L lost and HR drifting 137 → 150.
Meal: gamma absorption (τ 30/40/65 min), insulin secretion and sensitivity, hepatic output suppressed by
insulin, first-pass liver uptake, renal loss above 10 mmol/L — 75 g OGTT healthy peak 8.8 at 39 min,
5.6 at 2 h (WHO normal < 7.8); type 2 11.6 at 2 h (≥ 11.1); type 1 > 18.
Motor units: 120 units, twitch forces over 100×, recruitment thresholds exponential to 50 % (hand) or
80 % (thigh) of drive, rate coding 8–35 Hz, fatigue ∝ gain² × size: Rohmert's curve — 50 % held 60 s,
30 % ~150 s, 15 % ~8 min; EMG and units firing grow during a constant-force hold.

Bugs found: a missing brace in the header turned the whole file into a syntax error in the VM loader;
the break set-up's fault leaked into every set-up (subject() read p.fault always); a relaxed fixed-point
for ventilation oscillated (slope −16 at rest) and left resting PaCO₂ at 44 — replaced by bisection;
the first meal model drifted down when fasting (uptake ≠ liver output) and peaked at 13 mmol/L at 18 min;
heart failure gave a resting HR of 112; "another person" control did nothing unless neurons were lost (now
shown only then); every set-up opened before anything had happened (per-set-up pacing; break opens mid-ride).

Upgrade pass 1: pure-O₂/CO₂/12 % O₂ breathing, plasma-volume drift and the kidneys in the heat ride,
type 1 with and without injected insulin, a walk after the meal, lost and re-grown motor neurons,
landscape plots (VO₂/HR/lactate against power with the lactate threshold; Rohmert's curve; VE against
PaCO₂ and PaO₂; sweat against air temperature; fault-to-healthy ratios). Upgrade pass 2 (after the
sheets): the glucose route drawn on the body's own anatomy instead of floating balls; flow bars fitted
to their plate; bench labels moved apart; pacing so each set-up shows its experiment within seconds.

## 6B-6 Stimulus, Signal, Response, Memory — `g6b-senses` (B6.1–B6.6)

Set-ups: `receptors` · `pathway` · `processing` · `reflex` · `memory` · `together`. 36 controls, 26 presets,
5 problems, 7 walkthrough steps, 5 quiz questions. Figure library `art-g6b-5.js` (skin in section with
Meissner, Merkel, Pacinian and free endings; receptive-field map under a caliper; hand, head and back on
the touch bench; nerve-conduction couch; choice-reaction board; brain in profile; knee-jerk and hot-plate
benches; the reflex arc; word grid and the three memory stores; a sports hall with catcher and thrower).

Models. Touch: two-point thresholds (Weinstein 1968: fingertip 2.5, lip 5.5, palm 10, forearm 38, back
42 mm), receptive fields threshold ÷ 1.6 apart, psychometric Φ((d − T)/0.22T); Weber fraction 0.05 on lifted
weights (75 % right at Δ = 0.05·I); nine volunteers whose thresholds differ ×0.8–1.25 (volunteer 1 typical);
receptor firing ∝ log of the pressure. Conduction: Aβ 50, Aδ 15, C 1 m/s, limb nerve plus cord tracts,
two synapses of 0.7 ms; cold limb Q10 1.6; demyelination slows myelinated fibres and blocks them past
85 %. Pin prick on the toe of a 170 cm person: touch 32 ms, sharp pain 104 ms, dull pain 1.45 s. Hick:
RT = 0.2 + 0.15·log₂(n + 1) s (compatible mapping ×0.35, practice shrinks b); 7 choices 0.65 s.
Reflex: knee jerk 80 m/s both ways, one synapse, 1 ms at the junction, 8 ms electromechanical delay →
23 ms at 170 cm; withdrawal three synapses → 56 ms at 160 cm; felt ~60 ms, a kick on purpose ~160 ms; a cut cord
keeps the reflex (stronger) and removes feeling and the voluntary kick. Memory: serial position =
asymptote (rises with study time) + primacy + recency erased by counting backwards; the DRM lure
"sleep" written ~44 % (Roediger & McDermott 1995); Ebbinghaus savings 1.84/((log t)^1.25 + 1.84) →
57/47/30/25/21 % at 20 min/1 h/1 d/6 d/31 d against his 58/44/34/25/21; a review multiplies stability by
1 + 12 × (fraction forgotten) — three daily reviews keep 45 % at 30 days instead of 21 %; meaningful
material ×10 stability. Catch: flight = d ÷ v against seven stages (retina 25, to cortex 30, judging 90,
deciding 50, to cord 15, to arm 15, hand 120 ms), practice shortens judging and deciding, a ready hand
×0.6, distraction +80 ms, seeing only the last 60 % of the flight. 60 km/h from 10 m: 0.60 s vs 0.28 s.

Bugs found: "another volunteer" moved nothing when a preset made the outcome certain (50 g + 10 g is
always told apart; 3 mm on the forearm never) — live.mjs caught it from the presets; fixed by giving
volunteers their own thresholds. The density readout rounded the forearm and back to "0 per cm²". The
"1 day" retention read 100 % because the test fell at the instant of a review (the test now comes first).
The walkthrough said 1 → 8 lights costs 0.45 s (the model gives 0.33 s) and that a beginner drops a
130 km/h ball from 18 m (the model, rightly, catches it); both corrected.

Upgrade pass 1 (after it worked): the lip and back are touched on a head and a lying torso instead of the
arm; the Weber test gets its own bench label and a receptor-firing chart showing equal ratios give equal
steps (with the same weight added to 50 g for comparison); withdrawal from heat on a hot-plate bench
instead of the knee; nonsense syllables or verse on the screen for Ebbinghaus; a sports hall with two
people, the camera framing the whole throw at every distance; brain-area labels that fit; a stronger,
spacing-dependent review effect and meaningful material; three "miss" presets and back/lip presets.
Upgrade pass 2 (after the sheets): the word list shows what was recalled at once, with a moving frame on
the word being shown and the three memory stores beneath (senses → short-term with rehearsal →
long-term, the lure "sleep" in long-term marked never shown); a cooled limb drawn bluer and labelled;
the camera drops to face height for the lip; overlapping chart and plate labels moved apart.
