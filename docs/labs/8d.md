Planned models and set-ups:
- **8D-1 `g8d-waves`**
  - Model: finite-difference wave equation y_tt = (T/μ) y_xx − γ y_t on a rope driven by a vibration generator. The far end is fixed, free or a matched absorber.
  - Slinky: longitudinal displacement u(x,t), with v = L√(k/m).
  - Ripple tank: the full water-wave dispersion ω² = (gk + σk³/ρ) tanh(kh).
  - Set-ups:
    - properties D1.1: A, λ, f and T read off a ruler and a strobe.
    - speed D1.2: T = mg over a pulley and μ from the rope type. A pulse is timed between two gates. v = fλ shows that f changes λ, not v.
    - energy D1.3: power −T y_x y_t integrated from the grid, P = ½μω²A²v. Doubling A gives 4× the energy.
    - types D1.4: rope vs slinky vs the orbits at a water surface.
    - data D1.5: ripple tank. Depth and frequency give λ, and v is compared with √(gh).
  - Published numbers: v = √(T/μ); shallow-water v = √(gh); the deep-water ripple minimum of 0.23 m/s at 1.7 cm.
- **8D-2 `g8d-light-matter`**
  - Models:
    - Fresnel R (mean of s and p) and Beer–Lambert absorption.
    - Colour seen = spectral reflectance × illuminant × CIE 1931 CMFs (Wyman–Sloan–Shirley fit) → sRGB. Illuminants: Planck A at 2856 K, D65, LED, sodium 589 nm.
    - Snell's law and TIR on a semicircular block (critical angle: glass 41.8°, water 48.8°).
  - Set-ups: outcomes D2.1 · model D2.2 · colour D2.3 · refraction D2.4 · evidence D2.5 (identify a mystery sample).
- **8D-3 `g8d-em-spectrum`**
  - Model: c = fλ, E = hf.
  - Set-ups:
    - visible D3.1: the prism.
    - order D3.2: ordering the bands.
    - uses D3.3: a ½-wave dipole of 1.5 m at 100 MHz; microwave hot spots in chocolate at 2.45 GHz, λ/2 = 6.1 cm, which measures c; a thermal camera with the 310 K Planck curve, Wien peak 9.35 µm; X-ray absorption e^{−μx}.
    - speed D3.4: lunar laser ranging, 2.56 s round trip; every band has the same speed in vacuum.
    - energy D3.5: photon eV against bond energies; UV and sunburn.
- **8D-4 `g8d-sound`**
  - medium D4.1: the bell jar. Radiated power ∝ ρc, so the level falls by 20·log10 of the pressure ratio, down to a floor set by the mounting.
  - pitchloud D4.2: pitch and loudness, dB SPL re 20 µPa, the inverse square law.
  - speed D4.3: 331.3√(1+T/273.15) in air, √(K/ρ) in liquids, √(E/ρ) in solids, measured by a two-microphone delay.
  - ear D4.4: the hearing threshold (Terhardt) and presbycusis.
  - echo D4.5: echo ranging d = vt/2, and an ultrasound A-scan with R = ((Z2−Z1)/(Z2+Z1))².
- **8D-5 `g8d-signals`**
  - D5.1: sampling and Nyquist; quantization SNR = 6.02N + 1.76 dB.
  - D5.2/D5.4: attenuation in dB/km. Analog SNR fades; digital BER = ½erfc(√SNR) holds, then falls off a cliff; repeaters.
  - D5.3: Hamming(7,4) correcting a single flipped bit.
  - D5.5: a light-link communicator. An LED and a photodiode with the inverse square law and noise; the bit rate sets the BER.
