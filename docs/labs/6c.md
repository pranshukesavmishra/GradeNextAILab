## 6C-1 · The Energy Chain Bench — `g6c-energy-chain` (C1)

Set-ups (6): `forms` C1.1 · `kinetic` C1.2 · `potential` C1.3 · `chain` C1.4 + C1.1 ·
`conserve` C1.5 · `everyday` C1.6 + C1.4. 34 controls (shown by set-up/device), 17 presets,
7 problems, 7 walkthrough steps, 5 quiz questions.

**Model.** The cart is integrated along the track's arc length (midpoint step, 0.5 ms): gravity,
rolling resistance μN with N including the curve's centripetal part, drag ½ρC_dAv², eddy brake bv,
clay yield 60 N. Light gates time a flag (v = flag/Δt). The chain steps sunlight → panel →
battery → motor (3 W) → pulley → mass → generator → lamp with real efficiencies. Kettle: lumped
water + 550 g body, side losses 1.6 W/K, evaporation by mass transfer (h_m 1 cm/s). Bike: constant
brake deceleration + drag + rolling. Phone: CC to 80 % then taper, charger η, cable I²R.

**Numbers reproduced** (all in `ms/g6c.test.ts`):
- ½kx² = 0.50 J → √(2E/m) = 1.41 m/s; the gate reads 1.389 m/s (losses ≈ 3 %).
- v = √(2gh): 2.426 m/s from 0.30 m (gate 2.400), independent of mass; Moon 0.986 m/s (gate 0.982).
- Ledger KE + PE + spring + thermal = start to < 0.1 % in every case (after the fix below, to 1e-5).
- Chain: 0.21·0.95·0.78·0.9·0.9·0.78·0.35 = 3.44 %; in = stored + light + heat.
- Kettle 1 L, 20→100 °C, 2 kW: mcΔT/P = 167 s; model 185 s (≈ 3 min, 91 %).
- Bike 80 kg at 8 m/s: 2560 J; disc rotor +35.5 K.

**Bugs found.** (1) Explicit Euler drifted the valley ledger by +3.5 % over 30 s — replaced by a
midpoint step; ledger now exact. (2) First kettle evaporation term was dimensionally wrong (105 kJ
with the lid on) — rewritten as vapour-density mass transfer. (3) Heights were offset by the
track's deck height in two readouts and the equation (start mgh, ledger) — removed. (4) Leaked
`textAlign` from R3 labels centred the card titles. (5) Camera did not re-home when the device
changed.
