## 6D-1 · The Water Cycle Machine — `g6d-water-cycle` (D1.1–D1.6)

Six set-ups, 28 presets, 8 problems, 31 controls (each set-up shows 3–10 of them).

| Set-up | Teaches | The model |
|---|---|---|
| reservoirs | D1.1 | Gleick (1996) inventory poured to scale into cylinders and a spot plate; land-ice melt → sea level from published sea-level equivalents (Fretwell 2013, Morlighem 2017, Farinotti 2019) |
| evaporate | D1.2 | sealed chamber: dish energy balance (hot plate − L·E − convection − radiation − sides); E = h_m·A·(a_w ρsat(T_w) − ρ_air), h_m from Chilton–Colburn; Buck (1996) saturation; condensation on lid and walls; supersaturation → cloud of drops that settle; lid film drips to a cylinder; molecular view (leaving ∝ ρsat at the surface, returning ∝ ρ_air) |
| rain | D1.3 | Green–Ampt on Rawls, Brakensiek & Miller (1983) soils; Kamphorst (2000) depression storage from roughness and slope; Manning sheet flow off the lip; Marshall–Palmer median drop size; Atlas (1973) terminal speed |
| transpire | D1.4 | Jarvis stomatal conductance (light, VPD), Campbell–Norman boundary layer, leaf energy balance; stomatal densities per side (Weiss); lamp light ∝ 1/d²; bubble v = E·A/πr²; bag humidity box |
| drivers | D1.5, D1.6 | world box model on Trenberth et al. (2007): ocean 413 + land 73 = 486 thousand km³/yr; evaporation ∝ sunlight, precipitation ∝ √g, drainage/rivers/glacier flow need gravity; exact steady state |
| residence | D1.6 | well-mixed lake, published V and retention τ (EPA Great Lakes Atlas, CIPEL, TERC); evaporation leaves dye and salt behind; closed outlet → salt accumulates |

Published numbers reproduced (tests in `app/src/curriculum/ms/g6d.test.ts`):
- 965.4 mL of ocean and 25.3 mL fresh per litre; fresh 2.53 %; air residence 9.7 days; all land ice → +65.6 m.
- e_s(20 °C) = 2.34 kPa, e_s(100 °C) = 101.3 kPa, ρsat(20 °C) = 17.3 g/m³, dew point of 20 °C / 50 % = 9.3 °C.
- Chow, Maidment & Mays Ex. 4.4.1: silty clay, Se 0.4 — ponds at 0.39 h (Fp 3.9 mm) at 1 cm/h, 0.042 h at 3 cm/h.
- Atlas: a 2 mm drop falls at 6.5 m/s. Latent heat of global evaporation 74 W/m².
- Lake Erie τ = 2.6 yr (dye at 2τ = 13.5 %), Superior 191 yr.

Bugs found while building:
- Melting all ice first gave 61 m (volume ÷ ocean area × an ad-hoc factor); replaced by the published sea-level equivalents (65.6 m).
- The box model was not steady at today's settings (snow fraction taken from land rain, run-off not balanced): soil and ice drifted. Rebalanced so every box is exactly steady (rivers 37.5 + glaciers 2.5 = 40 back to the sea); latent-heat readout was 1000× low (km³ → kg).
- Lake model first split the "through" flux with guessed evaporation shares; replaced by τ = V/outflow (as published) plus A·e evaporation.
- Ponding never happened on the tray (Manning drained every film): added depression storage (Kamphorst), which also makes slope matter.
- The dish needed ~25 min to warm, so nothing happened in a run: it now starts at its own steady temperature (bisection on the energy balance), as a pre-warmed dish would.

Upgrade pass 1 (after it worked): molecular two-way-traffic view of the water surface (display select);
pre-warmed dish and 5-minute time-lapse so the cloud and the drip appear in the first seconds; leaves roll
their blades toward the room (they read edge-on before); fewer, larger leaves on a shorter shoot; lamp distance
(1/d²) replaces a bare light slider.

Upgrade pass 2 (after the contact sheets): closer cameras on the chamber, the rain rig and the potometer;
condensate film threshold 15 g/m² so the first drips show sooner; drop card at ×6 (the 5 mm drop was
clipped); stomata field widened to 0.35 mm; ocean cylinder sized 1 L not 2 L; on a phone only the three key
labels are drawn, shortened leaders and a shorter stage hint.

Fix after merge (integrator's audit): the evaporate readouts read the chamber's air temperature, set only
by step(); a paused lab threw on `toFixed`. chamberStart/trayStart now define every read value. Lesson:
ship.mjs's mount pass steps the lab before reading; the DOM audit does not — every readout must be valid
straight after setup(). (ship.mjs now runs `audit.mjs <id>` too.)

## 6D-2 · The Atmosphere Column — `g6d-atmosphere` (D2.1–D2.5)

Five set-ups, 23 presets, 10 problems, 27 controls.

| Set-up | Teaches | The model |
|---|---|---|
| composition | D2.1 | dry-air mole fractions (NOAA/CRC), CO₂ from 180 to 1,000 ppm, vapour from humidity (Buck); absorbers in an upturned 100 mL cylinder over water: damp iron wool (O₂, τ 2 h, ×12 without vinegar, Q10 2), soda lime (CO₂), CaCl₂ (H₂O); a control cylinder; a 1,000-molecule card; the candle trap (Birk & Lawson 1999, Vitz 2000) |
| layers | D2.2 | U.S. Standard Atmosphere 1976 lapse rates to 84.852 km, stratopause warming scaled by an ozone factor, tropopause by latitude, Bates thermosphere toward T∞ by solar activity; hydrostatic pressure integrated in 50 m geopotential steps; boundaries found from the profile |
| pressure | D2.3 | Torricelli: h = (P − p_vap)/ρg for mercury (0 °C), water and wine; height from the standard atmosphere, weather offset, tilt; a flask weighed and pumped |
| ptrho | D2.4 | sealed gas syringe, PV = nRT; bath temperature relaxes (τ 25 s); bell-jar pressure; clamp → isochoric |
| rise | D2.5 | hot-air balloon: lift V(ρout − ρin)g, envelope heat balance (U 5.5 W/m²K over 4.2πr²), drag and added mass, standard lapse above a chosen ground temperature, 120 °C fabric limit |

Published numbers reproduced (tests): dry air 28.97 g/mol, 1.204 kg/m³ at 20 °C; 1976 standard P at 11/20/32/47 km
(226.32, 54.75, 8.680, 1.109 hPa) and its pauses at 11, 47, 84.85 km; 760.0 mm Hg and 10.11 m of water at sea level;
1.225 g in a litre at 15 °C; Boyle 72.3 mL at 70.1 kPa, Charles 60.2 mL at 80 °C, 122.1 kPa clamped; balloon 781 kg
for 2,800 m³ at 100 °C (the "7.9 kg per 1,000 ft³" rule), 76 °C to float 600 kg on a 15 °C day; candle out at ~16 % O₂.

Bugs found: the first profile added 54 K of ozone warming to every tropopause, giving a −30 °C stratopause in the
tropics; the stratopause now sits where the ozone puts it. The boundary finder skipped isothermal layers (reported
20 km, not 11). Flask air came out 1,000× too heavy (kg/m³ × mL). A z-integration with g(z) on geometric heights
missed the standard's geopotential numbers by 0.2 %; switched to geopotential, as the standard defines it.

Upgrade pass 1: the candle trap (a real experiment that teaches the opposite of the truth unless modelled — the
water rise is mostly cooling, the flame goes out at 16 % O₂); a control cylinder for the fair test; the
1,000-molecule card; the thin-blue-line limb card at true scale.

Upgrade pass 2 (sheets): cylinders and syringe drawn with stronger glass and a visible gas tint; the barometer's
mercury as a metallic column in front of its wooden scale (it was invisible against the tiles); a stairwell wall
for Pascal's 10 m tube; closer cameras; small readouts in exponent form; the balloon starts just past lift-off with
a ×20 time-lapse so every preset shows the flight; phone layouts keep the km scale and the tags on screen.
