## 6F-1 · Earth's Energy Balance (`g6f-energy-balance`, F1 + F3) — design

Set-ups:
1. `budget` (F1.1, F1.2) — the globe in sunlight + a cross-section plate of the climate system
   (air, clouds, ocean, sea ice, ice sheet, forest, desert, rock). Zero-D model:
   α_s from surface fractions (ocean 0.06, land 0.13–0.35 by cover, ice 0.65; ice spread over polar
   caps at φ = asin(1 − i)); α_clr = 0.065 + 0.62 α_s; α_cloudy = 0.32 + 0.3 α_s; α_p = c α_cld + (1−c) α_clr.
   OLR = (1 − ε/2) σT⁴, ε = 1 − (1 − ε_g)(1 − c ε_c), ε_c ≈ 0.542; ε_g = ε_g0 + 0.0436 ln(C/280)
   + 0.0106 (T − T0) when water vapour follows T. Calibrated at 280 ppm, c = 0.67, ice 6 %: T0 = 287 K.
   Targets: α_p ≈ 0.29, ASR ≈ 241, clear-sky OLR ≈ 266–270, cloud SW −47/LW +28/net ≈ −20 W/m² (CERES),
   no IR absorbers → 255 K (−18 °C, greenhouse effect 33 K), G = σT⁴ − OLR ≈ 150 W/m².
   C = ρc_w h × 0.71 (mixed-layer slider). Plot 2: absorbed vs emitted against T (the crossing).
2. `tyndall` (F3.1, F1.3) — Tyndall's 1859 tube: Leslie cube at 100 °C, rock-salt windows,
   thermopile + galvanometer; gas select (N₂, O₂, Ar, CO₂, CH₄, N₂O, H₂O, CFC-12), amount, path.
   Band model over the source's Planck spectrum, square-root (strong-line) saturation → log growth.
   Landscape: radiative efficiency × lifetime → GWP-100 (AR6: CH₄ 27.0 non-fossil from RE 5.7e-4
   W/m²/ppb incl. indirect and τ = 11.8 yr; AGWP_CO₂ = 8.95e-14; N₂O 273).
3. `jars` (F1.3) — two jars under a lamp, lids, gases, distance; lumped heat balance with convective
   loss (sealed vs open) and radiative loss through the gas. Trap: the sealed jar of AIR warms nearly
   as much — it measures blocked convection; Wood (1909): rock-salt and glass boxes both ≈ 55 °C.
4. `feedback` (F1.4, F1.5) — 1-D latitude EBM (North 1975 / Budyko): x = sin φ, s(x) = 1 − 0.482 P₂,
   ice below −10 °C (0.62) else 0.30; OLR = A + B T linearised about the baseline state so toggles
   change the response not the base: B_Planck 3.22, WV+LR −1.30, cloud −0.42 (AR6 Table 7.10),
   ice-albedo from the ice line, transport D (toggle). Tune A, D for ice edge ≈ 72° and mean ≈ 14 °C.
   Plot 1 T(lat), plot 2 the hysteresis loop vs solar % (snowball). Tests: Planck-only 2×CO₂ ≈ 1.15 K;
   all on within AR6 likely 2.5–4 K.
5. `causes` (F3.2, F3.3, F3.4) — 1750→2023: fossil (FOSSIL − CEMENT), cement, land-use CO₂ through
   the Joos et al. 2013 impulse response (a0 .2173; .2240/394.4; .2824/36.54; .2763/4.304 yr);
   CH₄ (τ 9.1, 2.78 Tg/ppb) and N₂O (τ 116, 4.79 TgN/ppb) one-box from emissions; Myhre 1998 forcings
   with overlap; tables for halogens+ozone, aerosols (AR6 −1.1 in 2019), land albedo (−0.2), solar
   (cycle minima list, AR6 0.72 ΔTSI(1−α)/4), volcanoes (−20 W/m² per SAOD; Krakatau, Santa María,
   Katmai, Agung, El Chichón, Pinatubo), ENSO-like AR(1) noise; two-layer ocean (Geoffroy 2013:
   C 7.3, C_D 106, γ 0.73) with ECS slider. Observed: HadCRUT5 decadal means vs 1850–1900 (2011–2020
   = 1.09). Tests: CO₂ 2023 ≈ 421, CH₄ 2019 ≈ 1866, N₂O ≈ 332, anthropogenic ERF 2019 ≈ 2.72,
   human warming 2010–2019 ≈ 1.07, natural ≈ 0. Stage: a TERRAIN landscape with power station,
   cement kiln, paddies/farms, clearing forest, volcano, sun.
6. `timescales` (F3.5) — kicks through the two-layer ocean + IRF: volcano (AOD), solar cycle,
   CO₂ pulse (≈ 22 % left after 1000 yr), steady emissions then stop, orbital. Stage: the two-layer
   ocean cutaway coloured by its anomaly, the driver drawn above. Plot 2: duration vs size of known
   changes (log–log) with the student's run as a dot.

## 6F-1 as built (2026-10-04)
Six set-ups, 66 controls, 28 presets, 7 problems, 8 walkthrough steps, 6 quiz questions.
Numbers reproduced (all in `ms/g6f.test.ts`): planet albedo 0.290, clear-sky 0.148, cloud effect −48/+28 W/m² (CERES);
no-absorber planet 255.5 K (−17.7 °C); Planck-only 2×CO₂ 1.15 K; EBM ECS 2.89 K (AR6 likely 2.5–4) with an ice edge at
66° and a mean of 13.7 °C; Tyndall's N₂O/CO₂ 1.84 (his 1.91); CH₄ GWP-100 27.0; Wood's glass box 55.6 °C (his ≈ 55);
CO₂ 2019 415 ppm (410), N₂O 332.5 (332), CH₄ 1840 (1866); human warming 2010–2019 1.067 °C (AR6 1.07); natural only
≈ 0.05; IRF after 1,000 y 21.8 %; peak warming 12 y after a pulse (Ricke & Caldeira ≈ 10); TCRE ≈ 1.3 °C/1000 GtC.

Calibration constants: BUD (ε_c 0.542, kC 0.0436, kWV 0.0106, T0 287 K); EBM A 212, B 2.0, D 0.44, ice 0.45
(cloud-masked) below −10 °C ±4 K, zenith term 0.09 P₂; JAR hb 6, hw 2, ho 4, kv 0.1; N₂O bands set for Tyndall's ratio.

Bugs found: (1) the Joos 2013 present-day IRF applied to all emissions since 1750 gave 446–461 ppm in 2019 — the sinks of
a preindustrial ocean are stronger; CO₂ now comes from the fitted carbon box (as 6A-3) and the IRF only shares it out
by source. (2) EBM base state snapped to a snowball: an explicit albedo step 0.5 K wide with dt 0.25 y was unstable;
widened the transition and dt 0.1. (3) A 0.62 ice albedo made 2×CO₂ melt every cap (small-ice-cap instability, ECS 4.4);
the cloud-masked 0.45 gives a gradual retreat. (4) Glass windows reported 8 % "absorbed" of nothing reaching the gas;
the galvanometer now reads absorbed × what arrives. (5) The snowball walkthrough said "stays frozen at today's Sun";
the model thaws at 95 %, so the text and presets now show the real 89–95 % window. (6) `mix` and `tint` read as dead
to live.mjs: the response time C/λ is now a readout; the tint colours by temperature.
