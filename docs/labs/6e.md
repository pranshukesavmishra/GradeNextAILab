## 6E-1 · Climate from Weather (`g6e-climate`, E1)

**Set-ups (5):** weathervsclimate (E1.1) · build (E1.2) · graphs (E1.3) · maps (E1.4) · zones (E1.5).
**Controls:** setup, station, station2, year, day, nYears, endYear, trend, seed, pace, mapvar, month, plat,
plon, iso, dT, pMul, units — 18, each scoped by `when`. Drag handles: the day on the calendar strip,
the record's bottom edge (N years), the map probe, station pins (snap to the nearest station), the
world thermometer (ΔT). 18 presets, 6 problems, 7 walkthrough steps, 5 quiz questions.

**Model.** Twelve stations with 1991–2020 monthly normals (NOAA NCEI, Met Office, BoM, IMD, MSS —
rounded). Daily generator: trigonometric interpolation of the monthly means with each harmonic
divided by sinc(πk/12), so every month averages to its normal exactly; AR(1) daily anomaly (φ 0.72),
seasonal spread (sdW/sdS), interannual σ 0.5 °C, linear trend about 2005.5; two-state Markov rain
(persistence 0.3), shifted-exponential amounts ≥ 1 mm; degree-day snowpack. Köppen–Geiger key after
Peel et al. 2007 (E tested first, Kottek 2006). World map: a geography model on the Natural Earth
land mask (zonal sea-level T, continentality from 2,500 km upwind, warm high-latitude seas; ITCZ,
monsoon, storm track, subtropical highs on west coasts, onshore trades, interior drying).

**Verified numbers.** All 12 stations' normals → their published Köppen class (Csb, Csa, BWh, BSk, Dfa,
Dfc, ET, Am, Af, Aw, Cfb, Cfa); 30 generated years reproduce each MAT within 0.3 °C, MAP within 10 %,
and the same class; Chicago single-year σ 0.65–0.72 °C, a 30-year mean ±0.12 °C (σ/√N); 2 °C/century
→ 0.60 °C between the 1961–90 and 1991–2020 normals; Denver dry line 10(2·10.46+28) = 489 mm (BSk);
Mumbai monsoon test 100 − 2349/25 = 6 mm (Aw); Miami ×0.9 rain → Aw. The map model gets 9 of 12
stations' classes; Death Valley and Denver fail for want of mountains (rain shadows) — used as a
teaching point (walkthrough 5, eqNote).

**Bugs found.** (1) Summer/winter spread was inverted (cos sign) — Chicago summer days scattered
5.5 °C, records of 47 °C; fixed to 3.3 °C, record 39 °C. (2) Wet days came out 20–40 % low because
exponential amounts under 1 mm counted as dry; amounts are now 1 + Exp(μ−1). (3) A preset's own
station was overwritten by the set-up defaults on first mount (`first` branch ignored `pre`).
(4) Map model first gave Chicago a dry summer (Dsa) and London −1 °C winters: added summer
convection over land, west-coast weighting of the subtropical highs, warm seas keyed to the upwind sea.
(5) The station lawn's texture dropped cells behind the camera, leaving a black wedge; a ground fill
now runs to the horizon under it.

**Upgrade pass 1** (after it worked): the stations' own records drawn on the map in the map's
colours (model vs record at a glance); a far-side marker for a station the globe hides; desert
pavement and scrub at a desert station, evergreens in winter, no trees on tundra.
**Upgrade pass 2** (after the sheets): label callouts that flip side instead of leaving the stage;
calendar strip lifted clear of the hint; graph cards sized to the stage; 1° Köppen grid; thermometer
scale labels thinned on narrow cards; phone field of view widened.

## 6E-2 · Circulation of Air and Ocean (`g6e-circulation`, E2)

**Set-ups (6):** convection (E2.1) · cells (E2.2) · coriolis (E2.3) · currents (E2.4) · density (E2.5) ·
heat (E2.6). **Controls (25):** power, heatAt, ice, fluid, waterT, view, lapse · logDay, dT, sunLat · rpm,
sense, speed, aim, frame, scale · tau, beta, logR, width · TA, SA, TB, SB, Hd, lift · Dt, S0, iceAlb.
Drag handles: the puck's launch arrow (aim and speed), the westerlies arrow (wind stress).
22 presets, 6 problems, 8 walkthrough steps, 5 quiz questions.

**Models.** Convection: Boussinesq vorticity–stream-function–temperature on a 65 × 25 grid of a
40 × 15 cm tank, UNESCO density (densest at 4 °C), hot-plate flux, ice at h = 300 W/m²K melting at
334 J/g, surface loss 8 W/m²K, eddy ν = κ = 1.2 × 10⁻⁵ m²/s (×10 for syrup), upwind advection, SOR,
Thom wall vorticity, sub-stepped for diffusion and CFL stability; permanganate tracer particles; a
speed-vs-power landscape run in the background four tanks at a time. Cells: Held & Hou (1980)
φ_H = √(5gHΔ/3Ω²a²), n = round(90/φ_H) alternating cells, surface winds from the meridional flow
turned by rotation, u_M = Ωa sin²φ/cos φ. Turntable: exact inertial path turned back by Ωt;
Rossby numbers at 45°. Ocean: Stommel (1948) closed-form ψ with overflow-safe exponentials,
Sverdrup transport τ₀πλ/(ρβb). Density: UNESCO 1981 EOS-80, Millero freezing point,
lock-exchange front ½√(g′H). Planet: North (1975) diffusive EBM, A 203.3, B 2.09, albedo
0.32 + 0.16 P₂ (ice 0.62), D = 0.55.

**Verified numbers.** ρ(35, 25) = 1023.343 kg/m³; ρ(0, 4) = 999.975; T_f(35) = −1.922 °C; lock
front 11.4 cm/s; φ_H(Earth) = 28.8°, 14.4° at a 12 h day, one cell on a 100-day planet; u_M(30°) =
134 m/s; f(45°) = 1.031 × 10⁻⁴ s⁻¹; puck miss 18.4 cm = RΩt at 10 rpm; Sverdrup 18.4 Sv, boundary
current 50 km = R/β on the west only with β; EBM world mean 15.5 °C, peak transport 4.9 PW at 34.5°
(measured 5.8 PW), equator 56 °C with D = 0 = (Qs(1−a) − A)/B.

**Bugs found.** (1) The explicit EBM blew up (NaN) at a 5-day step: diffusion limit 0.4 C dx²/2D,
now ~8000 s. (2) Syrup's ν broke the diffusion limit of the convection step and the tank never moved
(NaN); steps are now sub-divided for diffusion and CFL. (3) Stommel transport read 0 — the sign of Ψ₀
put ψ positive; transport is now max |ψ|. (4) Convection was too slow to compute (rhoSW twice per
cell per step): density is computed once per cell per step. (5) The turntable's motor base painted
over the disc from low cameras: it sits under the disc and is now sorted behind it.

**Upgrade pass 1:** the speed-vs-power landscape from four extra tanks run alongside; the
streamline inset (marching-squares ψ contours over the solver's temperature); the Rossby card
(sink to gyre); a T–S diagram with isopycnals and the freezing line; sunlight-in vs heat-out bars.
**Upgrade pass 2 (after the sheets):** default time-lapse ×20 so the plume forms while you watch;
a fixed 8 °C colour scale so a still tank looks still; thunderstorm towers with anvils in the
cross-section; disc and puck kept visible between launches; calmer streamlines on the ocean, grass
and sand on its coasts; callouts that stay on the stage, none on a phone.

## 6E-3 · Making the Next Generation (`g6e-generation`, E3)

**Set-ups (5):** courtship (E3.1) · care (E3.2) · flower (E3.3) · pollinate (E3.4) · seeds (E3.5).
**Controls (30):** shortTo, add, display, females, choosy, risk, seed, pace · clutch, food, parents, cpace ·
flower, dissect, ptype, wind, relH · mode, bees, constancy, carry, pesticide, fracB, nFlowers, lapse ·
stype, swind, turb, sH, wing, jay, gut, nSeeds. 19 presets, 6 problems, 7 walkthrough steps, 5 quiz questions.
New figure library `art-g6e-life.js` (window.G6L): widowbird male (a keeled half-metre tail) and
female, great tit, nestling with its gape, black-shouldered kite, jay, song thrush, honeybee with
pollen baskets, buttercup and cranesbill, lily and grass floret in 3D for dissection, pollen grains
(lily, sunflower, grass, pine with air sacs, ragweed), samara, dandelion achene, acorn, cherry, tree,
grass tufts, woven nest, a nest box cut open.

**Models.** Courtship: Andersson's four groups of nine; female choice ∝ e^{βL/50} among k visited
males; daily kite hazard 0.004·risk·(display/10)·(L/50)³; 45-day seasons; expected values from 120
seasons; a male's two-season success against tail length computed in closed form. Care: chick-level
great tit brood, D = 430 f/(f + 0.5) caterpillars a day shared by begging (∝ mass) with no chick
over its need, logistic growth to 20.5 g over 18 days, death after four days starved, first-year
survival 0.32/(1 + e^{−(m−17.8)/0.8}). Pollen: Stokes settling, H·U/v. Meadow: agent bees (3 m/s, 4 s a
flower, constancy, nearest-biased choice, carryover fraction, anther depletion), self-incompatible
flowers, 30 ovules, fertilised 30(1 − e^{−0.15g/30}); wind mode by neighbour density. Seeds: terminal
v = √(2mg/ρCA), log wind profile, Ornstein–Uhlenbeck gusts; jays cache, thrushes carry for the gut
time while hopping between trees.

**Verified numbers.** Expected nests: shortened 0.37, controls 1.06 and 1.04, lengthened 1.54 —
Andersson's order and spread; best tail moves 80 → 50 → 35 cm as the danger rises. Lack's clutch: 9
eggs in an ordinary spring (Wytham great tits lay 8–9), 6 in a poor one or with one parent, 11 in a rich
one; 14 eggs raise a third as many surviving young as 9. Grass pollen 3.68 cm/s, ragweed 1.56 cm/s (the
measured ~1.5), all Re < 1. Samara 0.96 m/s (measured ~1.0), dandelion 0.39 m/s (Cummins et al. 2018).
Choice between 75 and 50 cm tails 73 %; a 75 cm male survives the season 54 %.

**Bugs found.** (1) Brood model: chicks were fed by begging weight but the food a full chick could not
eat was thrown away, so small chicks starved in rich springs and food made no difference; now shared
by water-filling. (2) Hatching asynchrony made chick 10+ start below the death threshold — every
brood capped at nine. (3) Meadow seed set saturated at 100 % for one bee; pollen loads and
fertilisation efficiency re-scaled so visits matter. (4) Lengthened males kept their lead at any
predation (hazard ∝ L^1.5); the measured cost of long tails needed a steeper (L/50)³.

**Upgrade pass 1:** the far-side nests slung between grass stems; the other parent waiting on a twig;
pollen puffs carried off the anthers at the computed fall speed; the stations' microscope card; the
second-season fitness curve with its best tail. **Upgrade pass 2 (after the sheets):** larger chicks
weighed to the day's mass, a closer camera on the flower and its labels kept apart, nests drawn into
the grass instead of floating.

## 6E-4 · Nature and Nurture Growth Chambers (`g6e-nurture`, E4)

**Set-ups (7):** environment (E4.1) · test (E4.2) · genes (E4.3) · compare (E4.4) · separate (E4.5) ·
coat (E4.3, E4.5) · hydrangea (E4.1, E4.4).
**Controls (25):** setup · geno · ppfd, photo, water, N, T · factor, confound · gpair, dim, bright ·
reps, noise, seed · pace · rgeno, Tair, shave, ice, weeks · cultivar, pH, sulf, phos. 21 presets,
5 problems, 7 walkthrough steps, 5 quiz questions.
Added to `art-g6e-life.js`: a rapid-cycling Brassica rapa (Wisconsin Fast Plant) with cotyledons,
lobed alternate leaves and a raceme of four-petalled flowers, wilting and stretching as the model
says; a clay pot; a Himalayan rabbit whose fur darkens region by region; a hydrangea mophead of
four-sepalled florets; a growth cabinet in 3D with its lamp and glass door.

**Models.** Each plant is grown day by day: dW/dt = 1.6 g/mol · (Psat/PPFD)(1 − e^{−PPFD/Psat}) ·
absorbed PAR (1 − e^{−0.65 LAI}) · f(T) · f(water) · f(N) − maintenance (Q10 = 2). LAI from leaf mass
and a specific leaf area that rises in dim light; f(T) a beta function (4, 24, 36 °C);
f(water) = 1 − e^{−w/(4 + 4.5 LAI)}; f(N) = N/(N + 40). Height with shade-avoidance stretching;
flowering at 250 degree-days. Five genotypes differ in one or two numbers (Psat, maintenance, leaf
thickness, height, anthocyanin). Each plant has its own lognormal vigour. Statistics: Welch's t with
an exact two-sided p (regularised incomplete beta), and a two-way analysis of variance of the 2 × 2.
Himalayan rabbit: skin T = Tair + (39 − Tair)·k per region; the cʰ tyrosinase works below ~33.5 °C,
dark share 1/(1 + e^{(T − 33.5)/0.6}). Hydrangea: dissolved Al 0.3·10^{6.5 − pH} mg/kg plus aluminium
sulphate; sepal Al 180·Al/(Al + 12)/(1 + 2P) µg/g; blue above ~40 µg/g.

**Verified numbers.** Standard chamber (400 µmol/m²/s, 16 h, 22 °C): 1.69 g dry mass and 27 cm at day
28. Daily light 23.0 mol/m². Fair test of light: 0.07, 0.93, 2.03 and 3.67 g, D against A t = 12.4,
p = 0.001. Reaction norms cross: at 100 µmol the shade type makes 0.19 g and the sun type 0.02 g; at
900 µmol the sun type 3.83 g and the shade type 1.05 g. The 2 × 2 (sun/shade × 120/800 µmol): genes 15 %,
light 59 %, genes × light 23 %, plant to plant 2 %. Rabbit at 20 °C: ears 27.2 °C (black), back
36.3 °C (white), a shaved patch under ice 12 °C grows black. Hydrangea at pH 5.0: 9.49 mg/kg Al,
79.5 µg/g in the sepals, blue; at pH 7.2: 0.06 mg/kg, 0.9 µg/g, pink.

**Bugs found.** (1) Plants reached only a tenth of a gram: specific leaf area and radiation-use
efficiency re-scaled to Fast Plant sizes. (2) The shade type lost at every light: dim-light efficiency
is now the same quantum efficiency for every genotype, each saturating at its own Psat, with thinner
leaves and lower upkeep for the shade type, so the norms cross as measured. (3) The p-value came from
a normal approximation and was wrong for few plants: replaced by the exact Student t. (4) Water
limited every chamber: its scale now grows with leaf area. (5) The equation line crashed in the
compare set-up (no single run); the notebook printed a meaningless row there. (6) On a short stage the
rabbit's size went negative and the ice pack's ellipse threw a page error in the ship's sweep; clamped.

**Upgrade pass 1:** the chamber's own display of its settings; the confounded chamber flagged in the
readouts; plant-to-plant variation and a new batch of seed as controls; reaction-norm curves under the
chamber means; the rabbit's ice pack and wall thermometer; a pH series of the same hydrangea across the
bed. **Upgrade pass 2 (after the sheets):** the four-chamber row framed so no cabinet is cut off;
chamber names clamped to the stage; a larger rabbit; hydrangea series moved clear of the big pot;
time-lapse at ten days a second.
