## 6E-1 · Climate from Weather (`g6e-climate`, E1)

**Set-ups (5):** weathervsclimate (E1.1) · build (E1.2) · graphs (E1.3) · maps (E1.4) · zones (E1.5).
**Controls:** setup, station, station2, year, day, nYears, endYear, trend, seed, pace, mapvar, month, plat,
plon, iso, dT, pMul, units — 18, each scoped by `when`. Drag handles: the day on the calendar strip,
the record's bottom edge (N years), the map probe, station pins (snap to the nearest station), the
world thermometer (ΔT). 18 presets, 6 problems, 7 walkthrough steps, 5 quiz questions.

**Model.** Twelve stations with 1991–2020 monthly normals (NOAA NCEI, Met Office, BoM, IMD, MSS —
rounded). Daily generator: trigonometric interpolation of the monthly means with each harmonic
divided by sinc(πk/12), so every month averages to its normal exactly; AR(1) daily anomaly (φ 0.72),
seasonal spread (sdW/sdS), interannual σ 0.5 °C, linear trend about 2005.5; two-state Markov rain
(persistence 0.3), shifted-exponential amounts ≥ 1 mm; degree-day snowpack. Köppen–Geiger key after
Peel et al. 2007 (E tested first, Kottek 2006). World map: a geography model on the Natural Earth
land mask (zonal sea-level T, continentality from 2,500 km upwind, warm high-latitude seas; ITCZ,
monsoon, storm track, subtropical highs on west coasts, onshore trades, interior drying).

**Verified numbers.** All 12 stations' normals → their published Köppen class (Csb, Csa, BWh, BSk, Dfa,
Dfc, ET, Am, Af, Aw, Cfb, Cfa); 30 generated years reproduce each MAT within 0.3 °C, MAP within 10 %,
and the same class; Chicago single-year σ 0.65–0.72 °C, a 30-year mean ±0.12 °C (σ/√N); 2 °C/century
→ 0.60 °C between the 1961–90 and 1991–2020 normals; Denver dry line 10(2·10.46+28) = 489 mm (BSk);
Mumbai monsoon test 100 − 2349/25 = 6 mm (Aw); Miami ×0.9 rain → Aw. The map model gets 9 of 12
stations' classes; Death Valley and Denver fail for want of mountains (rain shadows) — used as a
teaching point (walkthrough 5, eqNote).

**Bugs found.** (1) Summer/winter spread was inverted (cos sign) — Chicago summer days scattered
5.5 °C, records of 47 °C; fixed to 3.3 °C, record 39 °C. (2) Wet days came out 20–40 % low because
exponential amounts under 1 mm counted as dry; amounts are now 1 + Exp(μ−1). (3) A preset's own
station was overwritten by the set-up defaults on first mount (`first` branch ignored `pre`).
(4) Map model first gave Chicago a dry summer (Dsa) and London −1 °C winters: added summer
convection over land, west-coast weighting of the subtropical highs, warm seas keyed to the upwind sea.
(5) The station lawn's texture dropped cells behind the camera, leaving a black wedge; a ground fill
now runs to the horizon under it.

**Upgrade pass 1** (after it worked): the stations' own records drawn on the map in the map's
colours (model vs record at a glance); a far-side marker for a station the globe hides; desert
pavement and scrub at a desert station, evergreens in winter, no trees on tundra.
**Upgrade pass 2** (after the sheets): label callouts that flip side instead of leaving the stage;
calendar strip lifted clear of the hint; graph cards sized to the stage; 1° Köppen grid; thermometer
scale labels thinned on narrow cards; phone field of view widened.
