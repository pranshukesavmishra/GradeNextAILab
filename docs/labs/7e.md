## Lab plan (models and published numbers to reproduce)
1. **7E-1 Rock cycle** — igneous: JMAK nucleation + growth on cooling (t ≈ L²/κ, κ = 10⁻⁶ m²/s),
   Johnson–Mehl thin section under crossed polars; sedimentary: Stokes settling (graded bed) +
   Athy compaction φ = φ₀e^(−cz) (Sclater & Christie 1980: shale 0.63, 0.51 km⁻¹; sand 0.49,
   0.27 km⁻¹); metamorphic: P–T path, Barrovian index minerals, Al₂SiO₅ triple point 501 °C,
   0.376 GPa (Holdaway 1971), P = ρgz (1 GPa ≈ 37 km); identify: Mohs tools, streak, HCl, density by
   displacement (quartz 2.65, calcite 2.71), magnet, loupe; energy: 47 TW internal heat flow
   (Davies & Davies 2010) vs sunlight, box model of the cycle (sediment flux ≈ 20 Gt/yr).
2. **7E-2 Stream table** — grid flow routing + Exner with Meyer-Peter–Müller transport,
   Hjulström/Shields thresholds, Ferguson–Church settling for delta sorting, frost wedging (9 %
   expansion), rain pH 5.6 from CO₂, surface area on crushing, soil horizons with time.
3. **7E-3 Fast and slow** — dz/dt = U − K A^m S^n + D∇²z over 1 Myr; event deposits; superposition,
   cross-cutting, unconformity; outcrop → continent.
4. **7E-4 Evidence** — Bullard 1965 fit (pole 44.0° N 30.6° W, 57°); fossils (Mesosaurus,
   Glossopteris, Lystrosaurus, Cynognathus); GPTS stripes, depth 2500 + 350√t m (Parsons & Sclater
   1977); GPS v = ωR sinΔ; Hawaii–Emperor ages; history by reverse rotation.
5. **7E-5 Boundaries** — ridge, subduction (arc at slab depth ~100 km), Airy root, spring-block
   stick–slip (Burridge–Knopoff), boundary type from relative Euler velocity.
6. **7E-6 Resources** — arc porphyry Cu, placers, oil window 60–120 °C via gradient, Darcy + Theis
   cone, Central Valley subsidence (~9 m near Mendota 1925–77), formation vs use rates.
